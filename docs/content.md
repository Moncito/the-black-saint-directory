## Process — six stations

1. **Discover** — A call, a brief, the references you love, and one clear measure of success.
2. **Strategy** — Sitemap, content plan, and an honest call on whether you need 3D. Quote signed.
3. **Design** — Moodboard, then key screens. Nothing gets built until you approve it.
4. **Build** — We develop, connect your content, and share a live staging link as we go.
5. **QA** — Tested on real phones, cheap and expensive, for speed, accessibility and search.
6. **Launch & Care** — We go live, hand over everything, and stay on to keep it fast and secure.
