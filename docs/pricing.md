## Quote builder logic (suggested)

- Inputs: service line, page count (for sites), 3D level (none / one scene / flagship), timeline (standard / rush), care plan.
- Rush timeline: ×1.2 on the range.
- Output: low–high range, timeline estimate, recommended care plan, and a "Send this to us" button that submits the selections with name, email, company and a message.
- Payment terms shown under the result: under ₱150k → 50% to start, 50% before launch; ₱150k+ → 40 / 30 / 30.
